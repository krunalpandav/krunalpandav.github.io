Nothing leaves your phone

No account, no sync, no server that can be breached. The vault is encrypted right here, and PassVault ships with no analytics, ads or crash-reporting code.

  • Zero-knowledge. Your master password is never stored. It unlocks a random vault key; every item is sealed with AES-256-GCM.
  • Hardware-backed keys. Settings are encrypted with a key held in the Android Keystore, and biometric unlock uses your phone's secure hardware.
  • Locks itself. Auto-lock after the time you choose, copied secrets cleared from the clipboard, and screenshots blocked by default.
  • Refuses unsafe phones. Like a banking app, PassVault won't open on a rooted phone or with USB debugging switched on.
  • Read in-house. QR codes are decoded by PassVault's own on-device code — nothing you scan is handed to anyone else.
  • Gone when you say so. Delete an item and it's gone; Settings → Erase vault wipes everything, including your Drive backup if you ask.

Questions

What if I forget my master password?

Use the Secret Key from your Emergency Kit to recover the vault and set a new master password. Without the master password or the Secret Key, nobody — including us — can open your vault.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no PassVault account and no PassVault server. Google Drive backup is optional and uses your own Google account.

What data do you collect?

None. PassVault never sends your vault, your usage or your device details to us. The privacy policy explains every case where anything leaves your phone.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes — restore from your Google Drive backup with your master password, or from an encrypted backup file with your Secret Key.
