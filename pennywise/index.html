What it does

Everything an ordinary month asks of a money book.

  • 📒

    Daily entries

    Income, expenses and transfers between your own accounts, each with a category, a note and a date.

  • 🏦

    Several accounts

    Cash, bank, savings — as many as you keep. One is your primary, and the app opens on it.

  • 🎯

    Monthly budgets

    A limit per category. Set it once and it carries forward; what was spent resets each month.

  • 📊

    Reports you can read

    Spending by category, income against expense, and budget against what actually went out.

  • 🤝

    A lending ledger

    Who owes you and who you owe, person by person, with the running balance kept for you.

  • 🔔

    Bill reminders

    Recurring bills, daily to yearly, with a nudge on the day — and a tap to record the payment.

  • 📄

    PDF statements

    Export a month or a year as a proper statement, laid out the way a bank would print it.

  • ↩️

    Carry forward

    Last month's closing balance opens the next one, so a month never starts from nothing.

The part most expense apps leave out

Why “nothing is collected” is a statement about the build, not a promise.

🔒

Nothing is compiled in to collect with

Pennywise has no analytics library, no advertising SDK and no crash-reporting service compiled into it. Not configured off — absent. We have no way of knowing how many people use the app, which screens they open, or a single figure any of them recorded.

That is possible because there is no account and no server. Your entries are written to a database inside the app's private storage on your phone, and they stay there unless you choose to move them.

☁️

Backup goes to your Drive

If you switch on Google Drive backup, the file goes to a hidden folder inside your Google account. The app asks for one permission, drive.appdata, which cannot read anything else in your Drive. We never hold a copy.
