This policy applies to the Pennywise mobile application for Android and iPhone, published by Ganutra (“we”, “us”). It does not cover any other app, website or service. Using Pennywise means you accept this policy and the Terms of Use.

1

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter — accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, the people in your ledger and your reminders — is written to a database inside the app's private storage on your device. On both platforms that storage is readable only by Pennywise.

Your preferences (theme, currency, reminder times, whether Drive backup is connected, and which Pennywise Pro plan this device last confirmed) sit alongside it in the same private storage.

Technically: a SQLite database named pennywise.db and a preferences file named pennywise.preferences_pb, both inside the app's own sandbox.

The backup copy on your phone. When you tap Backup data, the app writes one backup file, pennywise-backup.pennywise, to a Pennywise/Backups folder on your device, replacing the previous one. On Android that folder is in Android/media/app.ganutra.pennywise/; on iPhone it is in the app's folder in the Files app. It is kept there on purpose so that it survives the app being reinstalled — on Android it stays even after Pennywise is uninstalled (section 8 explains how to remove it).

Every backup file is encrypted. By default it is sealed with a key built into the app, which keeps it unreadable to anyone who simply opens the file, but not to someone who takes the app apart. You can add your own backup password in Settings; the app does not store or send that password, so if you forget it, a backup made with it cannot be opened by anyone, including us.

2

What we collect about you

Nothing. The app contains no analytics library, no advertising SDK, no crash-reporting service and no tracking of any kind. We do not know how many people use Pennywise, which screens they open, or anything they have recorded in it.

There is no account to identify you by, and no server for information to be sent to.

3

When data leaves your device

Only in ways you start yourself. There are exactly three:

  • Google Drive backup — off unless you turn it on. Section 4 covers it in full.
  • A backup file you share — the backup is written to your phone (section 1). If you share or move that .pennywise file, it goes wherever you send it.
  • A PDF statement — exported the same way, to a location you pick.

Those are ordinary files on your device, or wherever you send them. We never see them.

Buying or checking Pennywise Pro is not one of them: none of your records are involved. Section 5 explains what happens instead.

4

Google Drive backup

This is switched off until you connect an account, and it can be disconnected at any time.

When you connect, the app asks Google for a single permission — drive.appdata. That grants access to one hidden folder Google keeps for Pennywise inside your own Drive. It does not give the app access to your documents, your photos or anything else in your Drive, and it cannot be used to.

Once connected, you can choose to back up automatically — daily or weekly — and the app then uploads a fresh backup in the background whenever the phone is online, without you opening it. Choose Off to back up to Drive only when you tap it.

What is written there is the same encrypted file you would get from Backup data. It goes from your device to your Google account directly — it does not pass through us, and we hold no copy.

Because that folder lives in your Google account, Google's own privacy policy governs how they store it. You can inspect or delete it whenever you like, at drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Pennywise.

Disconnecting inside the app clears the connection on your device and asks Google to revoke the permission. You can also revoke it yourself at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5

Pennywise Pro purchases

Pennywise is paid for with a yearly subscription (which starts with a free trial) or a one-time lifetime purchase. Both are sold through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone, and the store handles the whole payment.

  • Your payment details go to the store, not to us. Card numbers, UPI handles, billing addresses and your store account are entered in Google's or Apple's own purchase screen. The app never sees them.
  • Checking your plan stays on the phone. The app asks the Play Store app or the App Store on your device whether this store account has an active plan, and the store answers with which plan it is. Pennywise runs no server for this, uses no third-party purchase service, and sends none of your records anywhere to do it.
  • The answer is remembered on the device, so the app keeps opening without an internet connection.
  • Sales reports. Google and Apple send us reports of sales, refunds and payouts so that we can be paid, account for tax and handle refund requests. Those reports come from the stores under their own privacy policies; we use them for nothing else.

A plan is not carried between Android and iPhone. Doing that would need an account and a server to match the two store purchases, and Pennywise has neither — section 5 of the Terms of Use explains what to do when switching.

Trials, renewals, cancellations and refunds are governed by the store you bought through. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy, and section 5 of our Terms of Use.

6

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications — to show the reminders you set. Decline and the rest of the app works normally; only reminders stop appearing.
  • Internet and network state — used solely to reach Google Drive when backup is switched on, and to wait for a connection before an automatic backup runs. With backup off, Pennywise itself makes no network requests at all; checking a Pro plan is done by the Play Store app or the App Store on your device.
  • In-app purchases (Android) — lets the app hand a Pro purchase to Google Play. It gives the app no access to your payment details.
  • Run after restart (Android) — so bill reminders and the expense reminder you have already scheduled survive the phone being switched off and on. Alarms do not otherwise outlive a reboot.

The app asks for no access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, SMS, call logs or your photo library.

7

Children

Pennywise is a personal record-keeping tool intended for general use. It collects nothing from anyone, children included, and has no account system through which an age could be known or a profile built.

8

Deleting your data

All of it is under your control, because all of it is either on your device or in your own Google account:

  • On the device — uninstalling Pennywise removes the database and preferences with it. On Android you can also use Settings → Apps → Pennywise → Clear data.
  • The backup copy on the phone — on Android it is not removed when you uninstall. Delete the Pennywise/Backups folder inside Android/media/app.ganutra.pennywise/ with any file manager. On iPhone it is removed with the app, or delete it from the Files app.
  • In your Drive — drive.google.com → Settings → Manage apps → Pennywise → Delete hidden app data.
  • Files you exported — delete them wherever you saved them.

There is nothing for us to delete, because we never held anything. That also means we cannot recover your data for you — see clause 3 of the Terms of Use.
