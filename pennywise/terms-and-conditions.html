What Pennywise is — and is not

Pennywise is a tool for recording money you have already spent, received or lent. It arranges what you enter and reports it back to you.

It is not financial, investment, tax or legal advice, and nothing it displays should be treated as such. Its figures are only as accurate as what you typed in. Before acting on anything with real consequences — filing a return, settling a debt, making an investment — check the underlying records and, where it matters, speak to someone qualified.

Pennywise does not connect to your bank, does not move money, and cannot make a payment on your behalf.

Your data is your responsibility

This is the clause worth reading twice. Because your records live on your device and nowhere else, they are lost with the device unless you have a backup.

  • If your phone is lost, broken, wiped or replaced and you have no backup, the data is gone. We hold no copy and cannot restore it.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes its data. Reinstalling does not bring it back.
  • Restoring a backup replaces everything currently in the app. It does not merge, and it cannot be undone.
  • Using Pennywise on two devices with the same Drive account means the most recent backup overwrites the previous one. Drive backup is a backup, not a sync.

Please keep a backup — a .pennywise file somewhere safe, Drive backup switched on, or both.

Pennywise Pro: free trial, yearly plan and lifetime

Using Pennywise requires a Pennywise Pro plan. There are two, both sold through Google Play on Android and the App Store on iPhone:

  • Yearly — an auto-renewing subscription. Store accounts that have not had one before start with a one-month free trial.
  • Lifetime — a single payment, with no trial and nothing that renews.

Prices are shown in the app, in your local currency, before you buy. They are set through the stores, may differ from country to country, and include or add taxes as your store requires.

Free trial. Starting the trial needs a payment method on your store account, but nothing is charged during it. When the month ends, the yearly price is charged automatically unless you cancel first — cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends to be sure you are not charged. The store decides who is eligible for a trial; it is one per store account.

Renewal. The yearly plan renews automatically each year at the price then in force, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. The stores tell you in advance about any price increase, under their own rules.

Cancelling. Cancel in the store, not in the app: on Android, Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions; on iPhone, Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. After cancelling, Pro stays active until the end of the period you have paid for. After that Pennywise becomes view-only: everything you recorded stays on your device, and you can still open it, read it, back it up and restore it, but adding or changing entries needs a plan again.

Lifetime. A lifetime purchase lasts for as long as we continue to distribute and support Pennywise, on the store account that bought it. It carries over to other devices signed in to the same store account, but not between platforms: a purchase through Google Play does not unlock Pennywise on an iPhone, or the other way round.

Switching between Android and iPhone. Every plan — yearly or lifetime — belongs to the store it was bought through and works only on that platform. Pennywise has no account and no server, so there is nothing that could carry a plan from one store to the other. If you move to the other platform, your records move with a backup, but you will need a plan from that platform's store. If you had a yearly subscription, cancel it in the old store so you are not charged twice; it stays active there until the end of the period you paid for.

Refunds. Payments are taken by Google or Apple, so refunds are theirs to give, under their policies. Request one through Google Play or at reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue a refund ourselves.

Your purchase is also subject to the store’s own terms: the Google Play Terms of Service, or Apple’s Media Services Terms and Conditions.
